# OE Chess user guide

## Opening drills

1. Open **Openings → Repertoire** and tick the lines you want for White, then for Black.
2. Open **Train**, pick a color, and play your moves. The app plays the book reply.
3. A wrong move is refused and the repertoire move is named. **Hint** marks the piece, then the arrow.
4. **Blind drill** hides the move list until you miss or ask.
5. Finish the line and the next due line is ready. Misses and slow lines return sooner. Clean lines wait longer.
6. **Play from here** drops the current position into a game against Stockfish.

## Endgames

1. Filter by piece, difficulty, category, side, or whether you have solved it.
2. **Random** and **Train weak** pick a position for you.
3. You play the side named on the card. Stockfish plays the other side at full strength unless you change that in Settings.
4. Mate positions end when you checkmate. Pawn drills can end on promotion. Defensive cards end when you hold the required number of moves or reach a draw.
5. **Hint** shows the engine’s move. **Takeback** rewinds. **Retry** starts the same position again.

## Backups and sync

- The trainer works with no connection. Games, reviews, and attempts are saved on the device.
- **Settings → Export local backup** downloads a JSON file. **Merge a backup** combines it with this device. Newer edits win, and nothing of yours is deleted just because the other file is older.
- Google sync needs an OAuth web client id in Settings. Sign in, then **Sync now**. **Restore from cloud** replaces this device after downloading a safety backup.
- **Export diagnostics** is the log to keep if something fails.

## What is not in the app

There is no puzzle rush, tactics set, online opponent, tournament, or chat.
